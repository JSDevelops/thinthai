# ThinThai Admin UX/UI concepts

Created 2026-10-08 with built-in imagegen. Eight desktop-oriented screens in four raster boards. No application code or real business data changed.

1. Overview and unified operations inbox.
2. Vendor review and food delivery area configuration. Final image corrects business category, filter, selected sidebar and uses ThinThai riders only.
3. Rider offer dispatch and lodging availability calendar.
4. Finance and scoped access control.

## Implementation constraints and review notes
All maps, identities, amounts, dates and KPIs are fictional. Use original project logo assets rather than generated leaf icon. Maintain one consistent sidebar ordering, including access to Services and vehicle services; generated boards show a condensed menu. Tablet adaptation requires drawer/column-collapse design and testing, not merely shrinking these desktop screenshots. Generated dates and weekdays need replacement with formatted real data. Avoid positive green growth indicators for increasing unresolved work. Different local/global date filters should have explicit labels.

Lodging calendar artwork extends the booking card across two date cells although it states one night: implementation MUST occupy only the night of20 October for checkout21 October (half-open date range), keeping21 available for another check-in. Booking, payment, refund and payout statuses are independent. Use actual reservation expiry/cancellation policies, not indefinite holds. Same sample booking appears pending in inbox and confirmed in calendar as separate example states.

Food area toggles affect food only, not shopping/trips/lodging. Store province/district/subdistrict separately from geographic delivery polygons. Rider offer is not assigned until accepted, with server-side race/expiry handling. Merchant approval requires checks completed. Scope permissions by role+area+service on server; hide unauthorized menus and restrict mutation/export, not just UI controls. Financial preparer and approver separation shown is an example workflow; apply configured limits and audit history. Approval/rejection, refunds and access changes require reason and review. Maps are illustrative and not route estimates.
